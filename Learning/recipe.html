Creamy Chocolate Fudge

Description

This creamy chocolate fudge is rich, smooth, and perfect for any occasion.

It's easy to make and will satisfy any chocolate lover's cravings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Combine sugar, cocoa, and milk in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly.
  2. Add butter and vanilla, then pour into a pan to cool and set.
